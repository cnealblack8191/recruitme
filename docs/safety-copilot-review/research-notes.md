## Regulatory / demand drivers (with sources)
- OSHA 2026 penalties: $16,550 serious / other-than-serious; $165,514 willful or repeated; unchanged from Jan 2025 because shutdown blocked CPI data. Small-employer penalty reductions expanded: max reduction now for 1–25 employees (11–25 went 60%→70%); 80% reduction on serious-willful for <=20 employees; history reduction 10%→20%. Sources: osha.gov/memos/2026-05-21/2026-annual-adjustments-osha-civil-penalties ; salussafety.io/us/osha/penalties ; bayareacompliance.com/resources/osha-fines-2026
- Georgia = federal OSHA jurisdiction for private sector (no state plan; GA public-employee-only plan). 29 CFR 1926 applies directly. Sources: osha.gov/stateplans ; lifesafetywiki.com/wiki/georgia-osha
- Georgia workers' comp: as of Jan 1 2026 required at 3+ employees (was 5), officers count, coverage within 30 days; fines up to $2,500 + $100/uninsured employee/day; construction WC $3.00–$8.50 per $100 payroll, electrical on the high end. Sources: onpay.com/insights/workers-comp-requirements-by-state/georgia ; zorninsight.com ; darwinfjohnson.com
- OSHA recordkeeping: 300A posted Feb 1–Apr 30; ITA electronic 300A due March 2 for establishments with 20–249 employees in high-hazard industries (construction is one); <=10 employees exempt from routine logs. Sources: coggno.com (osha-300a-electronic-submission-2026) ; basincheck.com/osha-deadlines-2026 ; osha.gov/injuryreporting/faqs
- Heat rule: NPRM Aug 30 2024; hearing Jun 16–Jul 2 2025; final rule not yet published (mid-2026) but OSHA says it will finalize; revised Heat NEP issued Apr 10 2026; proposed triggers heat index 80°F / 90°F, written HIIPP, acclimatization, breaks, training. Sources: osha.gov/heat-exposure/rulemaking ; ehsleaders.org 2026/07 ; bdlaw.com ; abccarolinas.org heat 2026
- Fatality/injury stats: 1,032 construction & extraction fatalities in 2024 (BLS CFOI); construction rate 9.2 per 100k FTE; falls 370 deaths (largest cause, -7.5%); electrocution ~8% of construction deaths; electricians among 5 trades with >32% of electrical fatalities; 5,180 nonfatal electrical injuries with days away in 2023–24 combined, +59% vs prior two years (ESFI). Sources: bls.gov/news.release/cfoi.nr0.htm ; esfi.org workplace stats ; constructiondive.com 812666
- GC prequalification cost burden: ISNetworld ~$875/yr baseline + size/training fees; Avetta $450–900/yr basic, $1,200+ higher tiers; hiring-client networks $300–1,000+/client/yr; small subs (MBE/WBE/DBE) priced out; docs required: training certs, COIs, EMR, 300A, written programs. Sources: billyforinsurance.com ; expiryedge.com avetta-vs-isnetworld ; getfileflo.com
- EMR: 1.00 = average; many owners/GCs require <=1.00, some <=0.90; >1.0 can disqualify from bids; safety program lowers EMR over time, saving thousands/yr. Sources: higginbotham.com ; buildforce.com EMR electrical ; cytrongroup.com
- GC safety submittals: site-specific safety plan before mobilization; competent person on site; some GCs require OSHA 30 for competent person when 3+ employees on site >5 days; documented inspections within 24h; weekly JHAs; toolbox talks; copy-paste identical plans are a red flag. Sources: powerconstruction.net Sub-Site-Safety-Requirements ; bigrentz.com SSSP ; occupros.com construction safety plan template
- Georgia Tech OSHA 21(d) On-Site Consultation: free, confidential, <250 at a site & <500 corporate; must correct serious hazards; SHARP recognition = exemption from programmed inspections up to 2 yrs, renewals up to 3; requires DART/TRC below industry average and >=1 yr operating. Sources: oshainfo.gatech.edu ; osha.gov/sharp/faq ; shepherdins.com SHARP
